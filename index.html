Online notary FAQ

A few common questions clients ask before scheduling a remote online notarization.

Before your session

Have your document ready, bring a valid government-issued ID, and do not sign until instructed during the session.

Can every document be notarized online?

No. Some documents may have special requirements or may not be eligible for remote online notarization. Eligibility depends on the document, the receiving agency, platform rules, and applicable law.

Do I sign before the appointment?

Usually, no. For many notarizations, you should wait to sign until the live session so the notary can properly witness or verify the act.

Do you provide legal advice?

No. EXPRESS NOTARY WA is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice, document drafting, legal opinions, or legal representation.

What do I need for the appointment?

You will generally need your document, a valid government-issued ID, a stable internet connection, and access to the online notarization platform.
