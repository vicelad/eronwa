FAQ

Questions before booking

How much will I pay in total?

A single-document appointment during standard hours totals $35: $25 for the notarial act plus a $10 booking fee. Each additional notarial seal is $10. Appointments outside standard hours may carry additional charges, confirmed with you before your session.

What are your hours?

Sunday through Friday, 6am to 8pm Pacific. Saturday is closed. Appointments outside these hours can sometimes be arranged and may carry an additional charge.

Can every document be notarized online?

No. Eligibility depends on the document type, platform requirements, and applicable law.

Do you provide legal advice?

No. EXPRESS NOTARY WA is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice.

What do I need for the appointment?

You generally need your document, a valid government-issued ID, and a stable internet connection.
