FAQ

Questions before booking

How much will I pay in total?

A single-document appointment during standard hours totals $35: $25 for the notarial act plus a $10 booking fee. Each additional notarial seal is $15. Witnesses provided through the platform are $10 each. Appointments outside standard hours may carry additional charges, confirmed with you before your session.

Do you provide witnesses?

Yes. Witnesses can be arranged through the notarization platform for $10 each, billed to you at cost as a non-notarial service. You are also welcome to bring your own witnesses to the session at no additional charge.

Do you handle estate planning and multi-document packages?

Yes. Packages with several documents are quoted in advance so you have a fixed number before the session. Email me your document list and the number of signers and I'll send an itemized estimate.

What are your hours?

Standard hours are Sunday through Friday, 6am to 8pm Pacific, and Saturday is closed. Additional hours are available at request, including evenings, Saturdays, and holidays. Email me with the time you need and I'll confirm availability and any additional charge before you book.

Can every document be notarized online?

No. Eligibility depends on the document type, platform requirements, and applicable law.

Do you provide legal advice?

No. EXPRESS NOTARY WA is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice.

What do I need for the appointment?

You generally need your document, a valid government-issued ID, and a stable internet connection.
