For attorneys & estate planning professionals

A notary your practice can count on, every time.

If you handle powers of attorney, trust documents, or other paperwork that needs notarizing on a regular basis, chasing down a one-off appointment every time isn't a great use of anyone's time. What partnering looks like

A simpler, more predictable way to get your clients notarized.

Priority booking

Requests from partners are reviewed ahead of the general queue, so your clients aren't stuck waiting on a signature that's holding up a closing or a filing.

Reduced, volume-based rates

Pricing reflects an ongoing relationship rather than a one-time booking. We agree on a rate that makes sense for your practice's volume.

One dedicated point of contact

You and your clients deal directly with me, start to finish — not a call center or a different notary each time.

Built for estate planning paperwork

Comfortable handling the documents that come up most in estate planning practice, including powers of attorney and eligible trust documents.

A simple referral process

Send a client's contact details once and let a consistent, repeatable process take it from there — no re-explaining how this works every time.

Still fully compliant

Every session still meets Washington State's identity verification, recording, and journal requirements for remote online notarization. The partnership changes the experience, not the standard.

Who this is for

The partner arrangement is built for anyone who needs notarizations for clients on a recurring basis, including:

  • Estate planning and trust attorneys
  • General practice and real estate attorneys
  • Title and escrow companies
  • Financial advisors and wealth management firms
  • Any practice or business that regularly sends clients for notarization

Note: E-RON WA does not currently offer loan signing services, such as mortgage refinance or purchase closings.
