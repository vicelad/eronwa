Eligible services

The same notarial acts we perform for individual clients, available under the partner arrangement.

Acknowledgments

For signers acknowledging documents willingly — the most common notarization for real estate, business, and legal documents. The document can be signed before the session.

Jurats

For documents requiring an oath or affirmation, such as sworn statements, affidavits, and depositions. Signing has to happen live during the appointment.

Trust documents

Support for eligible estate and trust documents, including revocable living trusts, amendments and restatements, and certifications of trust.

Power of attorney

For eligible POA documents requiring notarization — durable, financial, healthcare, or limited and special power of attorney.

Not every document within these categories automatically qualifies for remote online notarization. Send us the document list ahead of time and we'll confirm eligibility.
